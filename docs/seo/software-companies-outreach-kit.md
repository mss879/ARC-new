## 1. Tell the listed companies

**Why this comes first:** the 50 companies on the list are the people most likely to share or link to it, and asking them to check their own profile keeps the guide accurate. Ask for a fact-check, not for a link.

**How:** LinkedIn message to the founder, or the marketing or communications lead. Find the right person on the company's LinkedIn page. No contact details are stored in this file.

**LinkedIn message**

> Hi {first name}, I'm Shahid from ARC AI in Colombo. We've published an independent guide to software companies in Sri Lanka, and {Company} is one of the 50 profiled: {profile link}
>
> Everything on your profile comes from your own website, LinkedIn page and press coverage, and the sources are listed. Could you take a minute to check we have {Company} right? If anything is wrong or out of date, tell me and I'll correct it the same day.

**Email version**

> Subject: {Company} in our guide to software companies in Sri Lanka: please check your profile
>
> Hi {first name},
>
> ARC AI has published an independent guide to software companies in Sri Lanka. {Company} is one of the 50 companies profiled: {profile link}
>
> We built every profile from primary sources: your website, your LinkedIn page and press coverage. The sources are listed under your profile, with the month we last checked them.
>
> Could you look it over? If a fact is wrong or out of date, reply with the correction and a source and we will update it the same day. No company paid to be included and none can pay to change its profile.
>
> Thank you,
> Shahid Shamir, ARC AI

**If they ask how to mention it:** offer this, and say plainly that it is optional.

```html
<a href="{profile link}" rel="nofollow">Profiled in ARC AI's guide to software companies in Sri Lanka</a>
```

**Ground rules**
- Do not ask for a link, offer a swap, or offer anything in return for coverage.
- Correct real errors the same day and add a line to the page's change list.
- If a company asks to be removed, remove it.
- Keep a simple log: company, who you wrote to, date, reply, change made.

## 4. Google Business Profile

On every search I checked, a map pack of three companies sits above all the normal results. One of the three had 28 reviews, so this is reachable.

- Claim or create the profile for the Colombo office at the real street address.
- Primary category: **Software company**.
- Name exactly as registered. No keywords in the name.
- Add services, opening hours, phone, website and at least ten real photos (office, team, work).
- Ask every satisfied client for a review, and reply to each one.
- Post an update once a month.

## 7. Monthly upkeep (about 30 minutes)

The directory at the top of your target search stays there partly because it is visibly current.

- Check the Export Development Board's latest monthly release and update the export figures.
- Re-check ratings and review counts, and any headcount older than a year.
- Run the link check and fix or remove anything that no longer loads.
- Apply corrections from companies.
- Only then update the page's "last updated" date and add a line to its change list.
- In January, re-verify everything before changing the year in the title.
